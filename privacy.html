Privacy

Biblical Languages Trainer · Developed by Kletos Koinonia · Last updated 18 November 2026

In short: your progress and notes belong to you. They are kept on your own device and, if you turn on sync, in a private area of your own Google Drive. The developer never receives, sees or stores any of it.

What the app keeps

The app saves your study progress, notes, topics, studies, highlights, bookmarks and settings on your device, so it works without internet.

Sync with Google Drive (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google, the app asks Google for one permission only: to keep its own sync file in the hidden app-data area of your Google Drive (drive.appdata). The app cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files, and nobody else can see this sync file.

The app uses this permission only to save your progress there and read it back on your other devices. The sync file goes directly between your device and Google; it does not pass through any server of the developer.

What is never collected

  • No accounts with the developer, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Your data is never sold, shared or used for any other purpose.

Removing your data

  • To stop sync and remove the app's access: Google Account › Security › Third-party apps with account access, then remove Biblical Languages Trainer.
  • To delete the sync file: Google Drive (web) › Settings › Manage apps › Biblical Languages Trainer › Delete hidden app data.
  • To delete what is on a device: uninstall the app, or clear the site's data in your browser.

Google API data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
