# Biblical Languages Trainer

**Learn New Testament Greek and Old Testament Hebrew from the Bible itself — with a full Interlinear Bible, cross-references, notes, a reading plan and Church History.**

Developed by **Kletos Koinonia**.

👉 **Open it:** https://kletoskoinonia.github.io/trainer/

---

## What's inside

- **Vocabulary** — the most frequent words first, in sets of 20, with spaced review (words come back after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30… days) and an optional daily limit.
- **Lessons** — 12 Greek and 10 Hebrew grammar lessons, explained in plain words with real verses. Thousands of questions are generated from the biblical text itself, so every drill is different; a mastery test (85% to pass) and refreshers keep it learned.
- **Interlinear Bible** — the whole Bible: Hebrew (Westminster Leningrad Codex) and Greek (1894 Textus Receptus) beside the King James Version, the Tagalog Unlocked Literal Bible, or both. Tap any verse for a word-by-word study with Strong's numbers, Vine's and Brown-Driver-Briggs.
- **Matthew Henry's introductions** — to every book (with his dating and an outline by chapter) and to every chapter.
- **Cross-references** — the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge under every verse; open, copy or share them.
- **Notes & Topics** — notes on any verse, gathered into topics, exportable to PDF.
- **Reading Plan** — M'Cheyne's calendar or straight through from any book, at your own pace, plus Proverbs a chapter a day.
- **Church History** — from the Apostolic Fathers to the Reformation, in their own words.

Everything works offline once loaded. Your progress stays on your own device.

---

## How to use it

**On a computer** — open the link above in Chrome or Edge. To keep it as an app, click **Install** in the address bar. Chrome and Edge can also keep your progress in step with your phone through a sync file in Google Drive (Settings › Sync).

**On iPhone or iPad** — open the link in **Safari**, tap **Share › Add to Home Screen**, then open it from the new *Biblical Languages* icon. It runs full-screen and works without internet. Back up your progress now and then with **Settings › Back up progress**.

**On Android** — use the Biblical Languages app (ask Kletos Koinonia for the latest version), or install the web app from Chrome. The Android app checks for updates by itself.

New versions are published here; installed copies pick them up automatically and offer to restart.
